# Quick Reference Guide

## Preset Comparison Chart

| Preset | Contrast | Saturation | Grain | Tone | Best Use Case |
|--------|----------|------------|-------|------|---------------|
| **Japanese Soft Film** | Low (-15) | Low (-8) | Heavy (30%) | Warm Pastel | Portraits, Nature |
| **Tokyo Street** | High (+25) | Medium (-5) | Light (15%) | Cool Blue | Urban, Night |
| **Kyoto Moody** | Low (-5) | Very Low (-15) | Medium (25%) | Warm Earth | Temples, Atmosphere |
| **Ricoh Positive Film** | Medium (+20) | High (+10) | Light (10%) | Warm Bright | General, Travel |
| **Ricoh Negative Film** | Low (-10) | Low (-12) | Medium (20%) | Cool Muted | Documentary, Vintage |
| **Ricoh Snap** | Medium (+15) | Medium (+5) | Minimal (8%) | Neutral Punchy | Street, Candid |

## Color Adjustments Overview

### Japanese Soft Film
- 🟠 Orange/Yellow: Enhanced
- 🟢 Green/Aqua: Reduced
- 🔵 Blue: Slightly reduced
- 🟡 Split Tone: Warm shadows + Warm highlights

### Tokyo Street
- 🔴 Red: Enhanced, darker
- 🟡 Yellow: Bright
- 🔵 Blue/Aqua: Heavily enhanced
- 🟣 Split Tone: Blue shadows + Warm highlights

### Kyoto Moody
- 🟠 Orange: Enhanced hue, muted saturation
- 🟢 Green: Very muted
- 🔵 Blue: Muted, darker
- 🟤 Split Tone: Warm earth tones

### Ricoh GR Positive Film
- 🎨 All colors: Enhanced equally
- 🌟 Vibrance: High (+20)
- 🔆 Brightness: Slightly lifted
- ☀️ Temperature: Warm (+10)

### Ricoh GR Negative Film
- 🎨 All colors: Muted equally
- 🌫️ Vibrance: Reduced (-15)
- 🌙 Shadows: Lifted significantly
- ❄️ Temperature: Cool (-2)

### Ricoh GR Snap
- 🎨 Colors: Natural + Punchy
- ⚡ Clarity: Enhanced (+15)
- 🔍 Texture: Enhanced (+10)
- 🎯 Balanced across spectrum

## When to Use Each Preset

### Time of Day
- **Morning/Golden Hour**: Japanese Soft Film, Positive Film
- **Midday**: Snap, Positive Film
- **Evening/Blue Hour**: Tokyo Street, Kyoto Moody
- **Night**: Tokyo Street
- **Overcast**: Kyoto Moody, Negative Film

### Subject Matter
- **People**: Japanese Soft Film, Positive Film, Negative Film
- **Architecture**: Tokyo Street, Snap
- **Nature**: Japanese Soft Film, Kyoto Moody
- **Street**: Tokyo Street, Snap
- **Food**: Positive Film, Japanese Soft Film
- **Temple/Cultural**: Kyoto Moody

### Lighting Conditions
- **Bright Sun**: Positive Film, Snap
- **Soft Light**: Japanese Soft Film, Negative Film
- **Harsh Light**: Tokyo Street (embraces contrast)
- **Low Light**: Kyoto Moody
- **Mixed/Artificial**: Tokyo Street, Snap

## Adjustment Workflow

1. **Import photo** to Lightroom
2. **Set white balance** (crucial for film simulations)
3. **Apply preset** that matches subject/mood
4. **Fine-tune exposure** if needed
5. **Adjust shadows/highlights** for detail
6. **Modify grain** to taste
7. **Export** and share!

## Pro Tips

### For Maximum Film Look:
- Start with properly exposed RAW files
- Don't push shadows too much (keep some mystery)
- Let highlights roll off naturally
- Embrace grain (don't remove it)
- Keep color grading subtle

### For Ricoh GR IV Authenticity:
- Use 28mm focal length (GR IV's native lens)
- Shoot close to subjects (1-2 meters)
- Keep ISO moderate (100-1600)
- Shoot in natural light when possible
- Use snap focus technique

### Combining Presets:
You can layer effects by:
1. Apply base preset at 100%
2. Reduce opacity if too strong
3. Selectively adjust specific colors
4. Add local adjustments (brushes, gradients)

- **Double tap**: Reset adjustment
- **Pinch**: Reset all adjustments

## Common Questions

**Q: Why do presets look different on my photos?**
A: Presets interact with your photo's lighting, colors, and white balance. Always adjust to taste.

**Q: Can I modify these presets?**
A: Yes! Apply preset, adjust settings, then save as new preset.

**Q: Which preset is most versatile?**
A: Ricoh GR Snap or Positive Film work well with most subjects.

**Q: Do these work with phone photos?**
A: Yes, but RAW files give better results.

**Q: Can I use these commercially?**
A: Yes, presets are free to use for personal and commercial projects.
