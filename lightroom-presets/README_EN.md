# Lightroom Presets - Japanese Styles & Ricoh GR IV Film Simulations

A collection of Lightroom presets that simulate Japanese photography styles and Ricoh GR IV film modes for use with Lightroom Mobile and Desktop.

## 📦 Included Files

### Japanese Styles

1. **Japanese Soft Film** - Soft Japanese film aesthetic
2. **Tokyo Street** - Tokyo street photography style
3. **Kyoto Moody** - Kyoto atmospheric mood

### Ricoh GR IV Film Simulations

4. **Ricoh GR Positive Film** - Simulates Positive Film mode
5. **Ricoh GR Negative Film** - Simulates Negative Film mode
6. **Ricoh GR Snap** - Simulates Snap mode

## 📱 How to Import to Lightroom Mobile

### For iOS:
1. Save .xmp files to iCloud Drive or Files
2. Open Lightroom Mobile
3. Go to Presets → ⋮ button → Import Presets
4. Select desired .xmp files
5. Presets will appear in User Presets group

### For Android:
1. Save .xmp files to device storage
2. Open Lightroom Mobile
3. Go to Presets → ⋮ icon → Import Presets
4. Select files from storage
5. Presets are ready to use

## 💻 How to Import to Lightroom Desktop

1. Open Lightroom Classic
2. Go to Develop Module
3. Right-click on Presets Panel → Import
4. Select all desired .xmp files
5. Presets will appear in User Presets

## 🎨 Preset Details

### 1. Japanese Soft Film
**Characteristics:** Soft film, pastel colors, warm atmosphere
- Low contrast (-15)
- Lifted shadows (+30)
- Enhanced orange and yellow (Warm tones)
- Film grain 30%
- Subtle vignette
- Best for: Portraits, nature, warm atmospheres

### 2. Tokyo Street
**Characteristics:** Sharp, cool tones, high contrast
- High contrast (+25)
- Enhanced Clarity & Texture
- Crushed shadows (-20)
- Emphasized blues and cyans
- Blue split toning in shadows
- Best for: Street, urban, architecture, night photography

### 3. Kyoto Moody
**Characteristics:** Dark colors, heavy atmosphere, earth tones
- Reduced saturation (-15)
- Significantly lowered highlights (-40)
- Orange-brown tones
- Soft film grain 25%
- Round vignette
- Best for: Temples, forests, quiet atmospheres

### 4. Ricoh GR Positive Film
**Characteristics:** Vibrant colors, warm, balanced contrast
- Warm temperature (+10)
- Enhanced Vibrance & Saturation
- Medium contrast (+20)
- All colors enhanced
- Light film grain 10%
- Best for: General photos, travel, portraits

### 5. Ricoh GR Negative Film
**Characteristics:** Faded colors, soft, cool tones
- Low contrast (-10)
- Reduced saturation (-12)
- Lifted shadows (+20)
- Slightly cool tones
- Soft film grain 20%
- Best for: Portraits, documentary, vintage look

### 6. Ricoh GR Snap
**Characteristics:** Balanced, sharp focus, optimized for snapshots
- Enhanced Clarity & Texture
- Medium contrast (+15)
- Natural but punchy colors
- Increased sharpness
- Minimal film grain 8%
- Best for: Street photography, candid, everyday shots

## ⚙️ Customization

These presets are starting points. You can further adjust:
- **Exposure** - Adjust according to image lighting
- **White Balance** - Adjust for lighting conditions
- **Highlights/Shadows** - Fine-tune for specific images
- **Saturation** - Increase or decrease to preference

## 📸 Usage Tips

1. **Shoot in RAW** for best results
2. **Set White Balance correctly** before applying presets
3. **Try multiple presets** to find the style that fits your image
4. **Further adjust** after applying preset for perfection
